Rules

Minimum holding

100,000 $COMPANY

Wallets below it earn nothing. You earn from the moment you reach it, never retroactively, and keep what you earned if you drop below.

Expiry

7 days

If a wallet goes more than 7 days without claiming, buying, selling or sending, its unclaimed rewards older than 7 days expire to the protocol address.

Stock buying

20 IMD per round

Each claim buys stocks with at most 20 IMD (and never more than 0.25% of the IMD/USDG pool or what each stock's own pool can take), and each stock at most once a minute. Every purchase is also checked against Chainlink's price: if the pool's price is off by more than 3%, that stock waits for the next round.

Common questions

Where do rewards arrive?

In your wallet, when you claim. One claim pays IMD and every stock you earned.

Do I need to stake?

No. Holding at least 100,000 $COMPANY in your wallet is enough.

What happens if I sell part of my balance?

Everything you earned stays yours. Future rewards follow your new balance, or stop if you drop below 100,000.

Why did my stock rewards change size?

They depend on trading volume, when stocks were last bought, and the price each stock was bought at.

Can anyone change the rules?

No. The token has no owner and no admin functions. The hook's owner can only open the pool once and change the protocol fee address.

How are stock prices protected?

Each purchase must get at least 97% of the stock that Chainlink's price feed says the money is worth. The IMD it spends is checked the same way, against IMD's price via its ETH pool and Chainlink's ETH price. If someone pushes a pool's price, or a feed is out of date, that stock simply waits for a later round, and if it still can't be bought after 30 days of tries its share is paid as IMD.

What if a stock can't be bought?

If Robinhood blocks a stock for this contract, that stock's share is paid to holders as IMD instead, in the same claim. Any other problem (a paused price feed, a thin pool) just makes that stock wait; if it still can't be bought after 30 days of tries, its share is paid as IMD. You never lose the value.

Are these real shares?

They are Robinhood Stock Tokens: tokens tracking the stock, not brokerage shares, with no voting rights. They aren't offered to US persons, and Robinhood can block addresses.

Has it been audited?

Not yet. Treat it as unaudited software until an audit is published.
